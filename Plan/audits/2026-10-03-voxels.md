# Audit: voxel models, assemblies and the `voxel` write-side verb (2026-10-03)

**Scope.** Report only, at rev `00d09791`. Files covered:
- **Documents.** The voxel document (`crates/ludion-scene/src/voxel.rs`: parse, validate, the canonical writer, palette overrides, the analyses). The ops engine and upscale (`voxel_edit.rs`). The JSON intermediate (`voxel_gen.rs`). `kind: assembly`/`kind: part` (`assembly.rs`).
- **Engine.** The greedy mesher (`crates/ludion-engine/src/voxel_mesh.rs`), `assembly_mesh.rs`, the asset cache's voxel, grid and assembly entries (`asset_cache.rs`), the extract assembly paths, `bounds.rs`, the voxelizer's voxel solid, `ragdoll.rs`'s part sizing, and the model-sheet staging and framing code in `model_sheet.rs`.
- **CLI.** `crates/ludion-cli/src/voxel.rs` (`new`/`apply`/`upscale`/`diff`/`from-json`) and the voxel and assembly arms of `check_scene.rs`.
- **Gate.** `scripts/voxel-drift.mjs`.
- **Editor.** The Voxel Artist's document reader and writer and the assembly reader (`editor/app/src/editor/voxel/{doc,patch,save,assembly}.ts`), and the viewport picker (`editor/app/src/viewer/picking.ts`).
- **Docs and plans.** `docs/voxel-*.md`, `dev-docs/assembly-animation.md`, and the plans named in the brief.

`collapse.rs` is prefab save-collapse, not voxel code, and was left out. `voxel_sheet.rs` is the GI `--dump-voxels` instrument; diagnostics L5 already covers its panic. Claims marked [verified] were confirmed with the debug `ludion` binary on scratch copies outside the repo, or by a test that pins the behaviour.

## Overall assessment

The voxel stack is in good shape.
- **Meshing.** The greedy mesher is a pure, exact function with pinned order, exact integer and half-integer coordinates, and correct culling and winding. Assemblies reuse it per part, with no second mesher. Offsets, pivots and mirroring are single-sourced (`pivot_in_mesh_frame`) and consistent across the mesher, the overlap lint, the editor and ragdolls.
- **Write side.** The writing verbs validate before they swap. Single-cell edits are byte-surgical, with a re-parse equality guard. Every grid-building path enforces the 256³ ceiling. The drift gate is a byte comparison with no false-green route.
- **The one real defect** is outside the voxel crates. `render.bounds` reports every voxel model, assembly and assembly part as a unit box scaled by `Transform`, ignoring the grid extent. Agents and the editor's click-picker therefore see voxel content 4–32× smaller than it renders.
- **The rest** is write-side polish: comment anchoring under `shift`, whole-file diff churn from reshape re-emits, `apply` editing generated models, `diff`'s exit code, and lineup staging of `..` paths.

## Medium

### Y1 — `render.bounds` reports voxel models and assemblies as a scaled unit box, 4–32× smaller than they render · Medium · [verified]

**Evidence.**
- `crates/ludion-engine/src/bounds.rs:219-229` (`local_box`) maps `PlacedMeshSource::Voxel`, `Assembly` and `AssemblyPart` to `(-0.5, 0, -0.5)..(0.5, 1, 0.5)`. That box is then transformed by the entity's model matrix.
- The mesher lays one voxel = one unit (`voxel_mesh.rs:254-256`, `to_model = coord − size/2`). A model's object-space extent is therefore its grid size, not 1.
- `crates/ludion-engine/tests/bounds.rs:188` pins the unit box for an assembly.
- The GI voxelizer gets this right: it uses the real grid extent (`voxelizer.rs:254-258`, `Solid::Voxel::object_box`). Its own comment at `voxelizer.rs:378` still claims to follow "the same rule `render.bounds` reports per entity", which is false for voxels.
- `docs/running.md:90-99` sells `render.bounds` as the screenshot-free answer to "did it land where I meant" and does not mention the approximation.

**Why it matters.**
- This query is the North Star tool for checking placement without a screenshot. The editor's click-picker and selection overlay also consume it (`editor/app/src/viewer/Viewport.tsx:23-25`, `picking.ts`, including the 3D pointer's `worldAabb` raycast).
- Vigil's player assembly is 32 cells tall at scale 0.125: 4 units in game. `render.bounds` reports it 0.125 units tall, a sliver at its feet. A barrel (4×5×4 at scale 0.5) is reported 0.5 tall instead of 2.5.

**Failure scenario.**
- An agent asks whether an enemy is fully on screen. `on_screen`/`screen_rect` describe a box 1/16 to 1/32 the model's size, so a model mostly cut off at the frame edge reads as fully visible.
- In the editor, clicking the player's torso picks nothing, or whatever entity sits behind it. Only a click at its feet selects it, and the selection rectangle is a dot.

**Fix.** Keep the asset-load-free fallback (the phase-10 decision 5 rationale: answer even for a missing file), but use the real extent when it is known:
- For a voxel, take `[-sx/2, 0, -sz/2]..[sx/2, sy, sz/2]` from the asset cache's grid or mesh entry when it is cached.
- For an assembly, take the assembled `size` the same way. For an assembly part, take its part mesh's offset-adjusted box.
- Fall back to the unit box only on a cache miss.
- Correct the voxelizer comment, and state the fallback in `docs/running.md`.

## Low

### Y2 — A `shift` along y moves the cells but leaves the layer comments behind, contradicting the doc · Low · [verified]

**Evidence.**
- `VoxelOp::reshapes()` (`crates/ludion-scene/src/voxel_edit.rs:351-353`) is only `Grow | Crop`. A `shift` therefore takes the surgical path, and `shift` (`:1668`) never touches `state.comments`.
- `grow` and `crop` remap layer comments (`:1340-1356`).
- `docs/voxel-ops.md:96` lists "`shift` / `grow` / `crop`" as the reshaping ops. `:106` says a reshape "re-emits canonically and remaps layer comments".

**Reproduction (scratch).** Set a head cell at y2 with `comment y: 2 text: "y2 - the head"`, then apply `shift by: "0,-2,0"`. The cell lands at y0. The output says "surgical — every other byte unchanged", and `# y2 - the head` still sits above the now-empty layer 2.

**Why it matters.** Layer anatomy comments are how an agent finds its way in a 48-layer model (`voxel new` writes one per layer). After a vertical shift, every one of them labels the wrong layer, and nothing reports it.

**Fix.**
- Decide the comment semantics once: comments follow content, as `grow` does. Then remap layers by `by[1]` in the `Shift` arm, and report comments shifted off the grid as `dropped_comments`. A y-shift then has to leave the surgical path.
- Correct the doc line that calls `shift` a reshape.
- The scaffold's literal `# y<N>` anchors mislabel after any `grow`/`crop` remap (verified: `# y0` ends up above layer 1 after a one-layer `low` grow). Consider making the scaffold anchors index-bound and regenerating them on re-emit.

### Y3 — A reshape re-emit strips the quotes from every letter-led row, so a one-layer `grow` rewrites untouched rows · Low · [verified]

**Evidence.**
- `emit_with_comments` writes each row through `scalar_string` (`crates/ludion-scene/src/voxel.rs:2144`). Its `plain_safe` rule (`scalar.rs:56-85`) leaves `AA` and `A.` bare and quotes only rows that start with `.` or a symbol.
- Every hand-authored and committed model quotes every row (`docs/voxel-format.md:229` tells authors to; for example `games/vigil/scenes/voxels/figure.voxel.yaml`, `barrel.voxel.yaml`).

**Reproduction (scratch).** A 2×2×2 model with all rows quoted, then `grow high: "0,1,0"` (one empty layer on top). The diff rewrites three untouched rows (`"AA"` → `AA`, `"A."` → `A.`) beside the added layer.

**Why it matters.** Pillar 4 asks for a minimal diff. Here the reviewable change (one layer) is buried in quote churn proportional to the model. It also makes a surgically edited file differ from what the canonical writer would emit for the same grid.

**Fix.** Always double-quote slice rows in `emit_with_comments`. A row is grid data, not prose; this matches the documented form and every committed file. `from-json` output is unaffected for today's generated models, whose rows all start with `.` and are already quoted. Re-check `voxel-drift` after the change.

### Y4 — `voxel apply` edits a `generated_from:` model without a word · Low · [verified]

**Evidence.**
- `run_apply` (`crates/ludion-cli/src/voxel.rs:127-206`) and `voxel_edit::apply` (`voxel_edit.rs:1162`) never consult `doc.generated_from`. `upscale` does handle it: it drops the key and says so (`:1881-1887`).
- `docs/voxel-generation.md:87`: "It marks the file write-only. Never hand-edit a model that carries it."
- CLAUDE.md: "Generated content is write-only for agents".

**Reproduction (scratch).** A model carrying `generated_from: gen.mjs` plus a one-cell `set` op. The run prints "applied 1 op … surgical" and exits 0.

**Why it matters.** The drift gate catches it eventually. Until then, the edit looks like a successful iteration, and an agent loses the loop to a gate failure that a one-line refusal would have prevented.

**Fix.** Refuse the run, or at least warn with an `L`-code naming the script, when the source carries `generated_from`. Allow `--dry-run` (read-only) through.

### Y5 — A model-sheet lineup of assemblies with `../` part paths stages colliding, or escaping, copies · Low · [verified]

**Evidence.**
- `copy_dependencies` (`crates/ludion-engine/src/model_sheet.rs:2685-2700`) writes `slot.join(file)`, with `file` being the part or clip path exactly as authored.
- Assembly part paths may contain `..`: `assembly_mesh::sibling` concatenates them, and nothing in `assembly.rs` rejects them.
- The lineup gives each model `lineup/<index>/` (`:2621-2639`). So `../parts/arm.part.yaml` from slot 0 and from slot 1 both resolve to `lineup/parts/arm.part.yaml`. The second copy overwrites the first before any cell renders.
- A deeper `../../../…` writes outside the `TempStage` root, which `Drop` never cleans up.

**Why it matters.** Two characters that keep their parts in sibling `parts/` directories would render in a lineup with the second model's limbs on the first. Or they fail with a confusing `L1138` fit error pointing at a temp path. The sheet is the human/agent look tool, so a wrong sheet is a silent false negative. No committed content uses `..` today (vigil's assemblies use `parts/…`), hence Low.

**Fix.** Choose one:
- Stage each dependency under the slot at a sanitized, root-anchored path (resolve against the assembly's directory, then re-root under the slot, rejecting anything that escapes), and rewrite the staged assembly's `file` keys to match.
- Or reject `..` in `parts[*].file`/`clips` with an `L1138` at load.

### Y6 — `voxel diff` exits 1 both for "the models differ" and "a model could not be read" · Low · [verified]

**Evidence.** `run_diff` (`crates/ludion-cli/src/voxel.rs:470`) returns `ExitCode::FAILURE` when the models differ. `read_model` (`:746`) returns the same code through `diagnostic::report` for an unreadable or invalid file. Scratch run: differ → 1, missing file → 1, identical → 0.

**Why it matters.** The doc pitches `diff` as something "a script can gate on" (`docs/voxel-ops.md`, `voxel diff`). A typo'd path then reads as "changed", the same signal as a real edit. `diff(1)` uses 1 for "different" and 2 for "trouble" for exactly this reason.

**Fix.** Return 2 on read or validate failure, and document both codes.

### Y7 — Stale doc comments around the voxel views and the GI bounds rule · Low · [verified]

**Evidence.**
- `crates/ludion-cli/src/check_scene.rs:16-17` says the voxel-view flags "apply only to `kind: voxel` documents". The same file's `:82-85`, the dispatcher (`:133-158`) and `docs/voxel-assemblies.md` all apply them to `kind: assembly` too.
- `voxelizer.rs:378` claims parity with `render.bounds` (see Y1).

**Fix.** Update both comments. The voxelizer's should drop the parity claim, or keep it once Y1 lands.

## Checked and healthy

- **Mesher correctness and determinism.**
  - Interior faces are culled against the neighbour in the face direction. Out-of-bounds reads count as empty, so the model's own boundary is always skinned.
  - Merges happen only within one palette index. Winding is chosen per sign, and normals are exact.
  - Positions are integers or half-integers, exact in `f32`.
  - Assembly parts are meshed separately (by design, so they can move). There are no cross-part culling holes, because nothing is culled across parts.
  - Greedy merging creates T-junctions at colour and size boundaries, as every greedy mesher does. They are coplanar or edge-aligned at exact coordinates in model space; no crack was observed or claimed, so this is not reported.
- **Empty and degenerate models.** `size` components must be ≥ 1. An all-`.` model meshes to an empty `MeshData`, which the renderer draws as nothing (`ludion-renderer/src/render.rs:1411`). The ragdoll switch refuses an empty part.
- **Memory ceilings.**
  - Every grid a verb or an assembly builds is held to `VOXEL_MAX_CELLS` (256³): `voxel new --size`, `grow`, `upscale`, an over-long `line`, the assembly `size`, and posed sheet grids.
  - A hand-authored document is bounded by its own text, because `size` must match the slices.
  - Palette indices are at most 94 (printable ASCII), so the `u8` index cannot overflow.
- **Validation UX.** Structure and data codes are split per family (`L1113`/`L1114`, `L1137`–`L1140`, `L1131`/`L1132`, `L1184`/`L1185`).
  - Diagnostics carry JSON Pointers with `~`/`/` glyphs escaped (`pointer_segment`), and nearest-match suggestions on unknown keys and parent names.
  - Part and clip diagnostics are prefixed with the part name and path.
  - Scene-level re-pointing goes to `/entities/<i>/components/Mesh/shape/...`.
- **Write-side atomicity.**
  - Every writing verb runs `check_voxel` on the final text, then writes temp-then-rename beside the target (`write_validated`).
  - `apply`'s surgical path is accepted only when the patched text re-parses to exactly the op result, which makes quote or escape hazards safe.
  - CRLF sources are handled (`trim_end` on row tails).
- **Drift gate.** `scripts/voxel-drift.mjs` discovers models by the `generated_from:` key (no manifest to forget), runs the generator, and byte-compares through `from-json --check`. A missing script, a failing script or any byte difference fails the gate. It runs in `check.mjs`, `ci.yml` and the private CI.
- **Caching and reload.**
  - Voxel meshes, voxel grids and assemblies are cached by normalized path, and hits are `Arc` clones. Extract dedups by path, so N placements make one upload.
  - Assembly mesh keys fold in the part hashes and the part index, so twin limbs stay distinct. Clip bytes are deliberately left out of the geometry hash.
  - The reload dependency map covers part and clip files, with a canonicalize fallback for `..` and absolute paths.
- **Pivot and mirror conventions.** `pivot_in_mesh_frame` is the single source used by `assembly_mesh`, the overlap lint, the editor's `pivotGlobal` and the ragdoll anchor. The ragdoll reads the root's derived world pose (`hierarchy::world_pose_transform`), not its local `Transform`.
- **No second mesher or palette validator.**
  - `validate_palette` and `emit_palette` are shared by voxel and assembly.
  - `voxel_gen.rs` parses JSON but validates through the same checker.
  - The editor's `doc.ts`/`assembly.ts` are total readers that defer every verdict to the engine. The editor's writes go through the host's atomic `write_editor_file`, and reloads go through `asset.reload`.
- **Vigil.** `heading_deg` uses the documented `atan2(-dx, -dz)` front convention. The player is a `kind: assembly`; enemies and props are single voxel models.

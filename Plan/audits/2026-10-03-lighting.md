# Audit: lighting and shading (PBR, textured PBR, shadows, environment/IBL, GI, post stack)

**Scope.** Report only; no fixes applied. Rev `6d2676fb`. Covered: the mesh pass
(`crates/ludion-renderer/src/shaders/mesh.wgsl`, `prepass.wgsl`, `shadow.wgsl`), the shared light
snippet and its CPU mirror (`shaders/snippets/lights.wgsl`, `cluster.rs`), lit sprites
(`sprite.wgsl`), the sky and IBL prefilter (`sky.wgsl`, `ibl.wgsl`, `ibl.rs`), GI bake and dynamic
injection (`gi_bake.wgsl`, `gi_inject.wgsl`, `gi_bin.rs`, the `prepare_frame` wiring in
`render.rs`), the post stack (`composite.wgsl` tonemap/fog/grade, `bloom_down.wgsl`,
`threshold.wgsl`, `ssao.wgsl`, `shafts.wgsl`, `snippets/medium.wgsl`), material maps (texture
upload, colour spaces, `mipgen.rs`, `material_maps`), the importer's material and tangent path
(`ludion-import/src/lib.rs`, `tangents.rs`), the light/environment/post components and their
validators (`ludion-engine/src/components.rs`, `extract.rs` `build_sun`), quality tiers
(`ludion-renderer/src/quality.rs`, `graph.rs` `PostChain::masked`, `ludion-engine/src/quality.rs`),
`shader_gen.rs` and its drift gate, and the docs `docs/lighting.md`, `docs/environment.md`,
`docs/materials.md`, `docs/quality.md`, `dev-docs/renderer-*.md`. Static reading plus throwaway
CPU-only scripts over committed mesh documents; no GPU work was run.

## Overall assessment

The lighting core is in good shape: the light-unit convention (premultiplied colour = diffuse
response at `ndl = 1`, specular × π) is applied consistently across point lights, the sun, the
IBL split-sum, the GI bake and the dynamic injection; per-slot colour spaces are right; clustered
culling is conservative and order-preserving; quality masking never leaves a pass reading a target
that was masked away. The one serious defect is in the import path, not the shader: generated
tangents carry the opposite handedness from glTF's convention, so every vigil town kit (none of them
export `TANGENT`) renders its normal maps with the green channel inverted, and the textured-PBR
fixture cannot see it because its ridges tilt only in tangent X. The rest are a shadow bias whose
world size is coupled to the shadow depth range (about 0.4 units at the defaults vigil uses), an
`Environment.ambient` that does nothing to meshes despite the docs, light components with no
range validation, and a suspected fp16 overflow at the roughness clamp.

## High

### O1 — Generated tangents use the wrong bitangent sign for glTF, inverting every normal map's green channel on meshes imported without `TANGENT` · High · [verified]

**Evidence.**
- `crates/ludion-import/src/tangents.rs:80` sets `w` so that `cross(n, t) * w` points along
  `tdir`, the direction of **increasing** `v` (Lengyel's method on raw UVs).
- glTF UVs have their origin at the top left, so `+v` is image-down, and the importer passes them
  through unflipped (`crates/ludion-import/src/lib.rs:1011-1013`). glTF's `normalTexture` convention
  is "+X right, +Y up" in the image, and `bitangent = cross(normal, tangent.xyz) * w` must point
  image-up (toward **decreasing** `v`). three.js documents the same trap: its
  `computeMikkTSpaceTangents(…, negateSign = true)` default exists because glTF needs `w` negated
  relative to the raw-UV result.
- The shader consumes the frame as authored: `mesh.wgsl:566` `b = cross(n_geom, t) * in.tangent.w`,
  then `mat3x3(t, b, n_geom) * sampled`, so `sampled.y` lands on image-down.
- Measured on committed content: a script over
  `games/vigil/assets/town/street-ground/meshes/cobble-4x4-a.mesh.yaml` found the bitangent along
  `+v` (image-down) on **800 of 800** triangles. None of the 18 normal-mapped kit exports in
  `games/vigil/art/town/kits/*/export/*.gltf` carries a `TANGENT` accessor, so every one goes
  through `compute_tangents` (`lib.rs:1082-1093`). The kits bake their normal maps in Blender
  (`tk.bake`), which writes the glTF/OpenGL +Y-up convention.
- The gate is blind: `examples/import-textured/make-fixture.mjs:122-123` draws ridges "tilted ±0.45
  in tangent x" only, and its authored `TANGENT` (lines 174-176) uses the same `+v` = up layout, so
  the authored and computed cubes agree with each other (both `w = +1` on the first face) and with
  the engine, never with a Blender export.

**Why it matters.** Every normal-mapped surface in the town reads with its vertical relief lit from
the wrong side: grout and mortar lines that should recess look raised under a light from above, and
the reverse. This is the content the Blender pipeline verdict (`Plan/asset-pipeline-blender.md`)
was chosen for.

**Failure scenario.** Light a cobble street from a torch north of the camera. The horizontal joints
between cobbles (normal tilted in tangent Y) shade as ridges, while vertical joints (tangent X)
shade correctly, so the paving looks embossed in one direction and engraved in the other.

**Suggested fix.** Negate the generated handedness: compute `w` against `-tdir`, or flip `v` before
the Lengyel pass. Add a fixture normal map with a tangent-Y tilt, imported once with and once
without an exporter-style `TANGENT` (`+v` = image-down), and assert both rendered cubes match. Then
re-sync the kits, which moves every textured golden.

## Medium

### O2 — `shadow_bias` is in shadow-NDC depth units, so its world size scales with `shadow_far - shadow_near`; at the defaults it is ~0.4 units and small props cast no shadow · Medium · [verified]

**Evidence.** `mesh.wgsl:293`: `compare = ndc.z - sun.direction.w * 2048.0 * texel.x`. The light
projection is `orthographic_rh(…, shadow_near, shadow_far)` (`ludion-engine/src/extract.rs:1625`),
so one NDC depth unit is `shadow_far - shadow_near` world units, and the bias is a constant with no
slope term (the shadow pipeline's `DepthBiasState` is default, `render.rs:4404`). At the component
defaults (`components.rs:488-498`: near 1, far 200, bias 0.002), which every vigil scene keeps
(21 scenes, all `shadow_near: 1.0`, `shadow_far: 200.0`), the bias is `0.002 × 199 ≈ 0.40` world
units at 2048², and 0.80 at `medium`'s 1024² (the `2048 × texel` scale).

**Why it matters.** `docs/lighting.md` says the bias is "authored against a 2048² map" and tuned for
"no peter-panning"; it never says it also depends on the depth range. Shortening `shadow_far` for
precision shrinks the bias and brings back acne, and lengthening it detaches every shadow.

**Failure scenario.** vigil's sun `[0.32, -0.45, 0.83]` (`games/vigil/scenes/budget-horde.yaml:82`)
has `|dir.y| ≈ 0.45`. A caster of height `h` shadows the ground beneath it only when
`h / 0.45 > 0.40`, so anything under ~0.18 units tall (≈0.36 at `medium`) casts no shadow at all,
and every wall or crate leaves a ~0.36-unit lit gap along its base.

**Suggested fix.** Express the bias in world units, dividing by `(far - near)` CPU-side when packing
`Sun.direction.w`, add a slope-scaled term (`DepthBiasState.slope_scale` on the shadow pipeline, or a
normal offset in the shader), and re-tune the default. Document the unit.

### O3 — `Environment.ambient` has no effect on meshes; the docs say it is added to every lit surface · Medium · [verified]

**Evidence.** Any `Environment` turns IBL on (`ibl.rs` `resolve` returns `params.x = 1.0` whenever
`env` is `Some`), and the mesh shader then takes the IBL branch, which never reads `lights.ambient`
(`mesh.wgsl:394-405`; `lights.ambient.rgb * albedo` exists only in the `else`). Extract feeds
`Environment.ambient` into `RenderScene.ambient` (`extract.rs:1184`), which reaches only the
sprite shader (`sprite.wgsl` `shade_two_sided`). Both `docs/environment.md:60` and the component
rustdoc (`components.rs:594`) say "flat ambient term added to every lit surface";
`Plan/environment-ibl.md:80` records that sprites keep it, but nothing says meshes lose it.

**Why it matters.** It is the only authorable ambient control, and it fails silently on the main
content path. An author (or agent) raising it to lift a dark interior sees no change and keeps
editing.

**Failure scenario.** A night scene with `Environment: {ambient: [0.3, 0.3, 0.3]}` renders its
meshes identically to `ambient: [0, 0, 0]`. Only lit sprites and tilemaps brighten.

**Suggested fix.** Either add `ambient × diffuse` inside the IBL branch (this moves every
`Environment` golden), or document the field as sprite-only and have extract warn when a scene with
`Environment` authors a non-default `ambient` and has meshes.

### O4 — `PointLight` and most `DirectionalLight` fields have no range validation; a zero `shadow_extent` or `near == far` puts NaN in the light-space matrix · Medium · [verified mechanism, suspected outcome]

**Evidence.** `PointLight` (`components.rs:434-445`) has no `SemanticValidate` impl and no
`schemars(range)` on `color`, `intensity` or `range`. `DirectionalLight`'s validator checks only
`direction` (`components.rs:515-536`): `intensity`, `color`, `shadow_extent`, `shadow_near` and
`shadow_far` carry no range. `build_sun` passes them straight to
`orthographic_rh(-extent, extent, …, near, far)` (`extract.rs:1625`). With `extent = 0`, glam's
`1 / (right - left)` is `inf` and `-(left + right) * inf` is NaN. `sun_shadow`'s guards are all
ordered comparisons (`mesh.wgsl:283-291`), which are false for NaN, so it samples the shadow map at
NaN coordinates with a NaN reference. `Environment` and `PostProcess` validate their colour triples
(`L1128`/`L1130`); lights are the gap. This is the same class as particles K3.

**Why it matters.** A typo (`intensity: -2`, `shadow_extent: 0`) validates clean. Negative light
subtracts radiance in the mesh pass, the GI bake and the injection. A NaN light-space matrix gives
driver-defined shadowing (wholly lit or wholly dark) that can differ between the two pinned
platforms.

**Suggested fix.** Add `schemars(range(min = 0))` to `PointLight.intensity`/`range` and
`DirectionalLight.intensity`, `exclusive min 0` on `shadow_extent`, and a semantic check for
`shadow_near < shadow_far` and non-negative colour components (the `L1128` pattern). Guard
`build_sun` for Rust-built values the same way it already guards `direction`.

### O5 — The specular lobe at the 0.045 roughness clamp exceeds fp16 range under ordinary lights; an `inf` scene texel becomes NaN in the Karis bloom downsample · Medium · [suspected]

**Evidence.** The scene target is `Rgba16Float` (`render.rs:60`, max 65504). At
`roughness = 0.045` (`mesh.wgsl:373`), the mirror configuration gives `π·D·V·F·ndl` per unit of
light colour of 60,966 for a metal (F = 1) at normal incidence, and 108,080 for a dielectric
(F0 = 0.04) at `ndv = 0.2`, rising toward ~950,000 at `ndv = 0.05` (computed with the shader's exact
`distribution_ggx`/`visibility_smith`/`fresnel_schlick`). vigil's lights run at intensity 2.0-2.4,
and the gallery authors `roughness: 0.0` (`examples/gallery/material-park.yaml`,
`night-sky.yaml`). Nothing clamps the shaded value before the store. If the backend converts the
overflow to `+inf` (IEEE round-to-nearest, as Metal does), `bloom_down.wgsl`'s
`karis_weight = 1 / (1 + luma)` is 0, `boxes[n] * w` is `inf × 0 = NaN`, and NaN spreads through the
pyramid into `composite`, where `max(NaN, 0)` is implementation-defined.

**Why it matters.** It produces sparkle pixels that turn into a block of NaN bloom, they appear only
on glossy content at grazing angles, and the outcome is backend-specific. Whether the store saturates or
goes to `inf` was not measured, which is why this is suspected rather than verified.

**Suggested fix.** Clamp the shaded radiance to a finite ceiling (e.g. `min(lit, vec3(60000.0))`)
before the store, or clamp each specular term. Also make `karis_weight` and the threshold pass
NaN/inf-safe.

## Low

### O6 — Lit sprites and tilemaps ignore the sun and IBL entirely; the docs promise "shading like any surface" · Low · [verified]

`sprite.wgsl` `shade_two_sided` sums `lights.ambient` and the clustered point lights only: no `Sun`
group is bound and no sun term exists. `docs/sprite-sheets.md:165` says `lit: true` "takes
clustered-forward shading like any surface", and `docs/lighting.md:66` mentions only that sprites
neither cast nor receive shadows. In a sun-lit scene a lit sprite reads as night-lit beside daylit
meshes. Either document it plainly or add an unshadowed sun term to the sprite path.

### O7 — Stale comments and docs in the lighting path · Low · [verified]

- `mesh.wgsl:155-160`: `uv` and `tangent` are "declared … nothing samples them yet / read by
  nothing yet". Both drive all five material maps and the TBN frame (`mesh.wgsl:547-569`). This is
  the stale-comment defect CLAUDE.md names.
- `docs/lighting.md:80`: "ambient is only `0.03`". The constant is `[0.05, 0.05, 0.06]`
  (`ludion-renderer/src/scene.rs:24`).
- `dev-docs/renderer-ibl.md:16`: points to "the `*_SIZE`/`*_SAMPLES` consts in `ibl.rs`". The
  sample counts live in `shaders/ibl.wgsl` (`IRR_SAMPLES`, `SPEC_SAMPLES`, `BRDF_SAMPLES`).

### O8 — The baked occlusion map does not attenuate GI bounce · Low · [verified]

`mesh.wgsl:417-452` multiplies `acc` by the occlusion texture, then adds `gi_bounce` and
`gi_dynamic` unattenuated. The comment's argument for leaving SSAO off the bounce (golden churn,
torch-lit interiors) does not carry over to glTF's `occlusionTexture`, which by definition darkens
all indirect light. Painted cavities therefore glow under bounce light. Low, because the GI bounce
is opt-in and small; fold the occlusion factor into the bounce terms when the GI goldens next move.

## Checked and healthy

- **Light units are consistent across consumers.** Point and sun lobes (`mesh.wgsl:473-514`), the
  IBL irradiance stored as a cosine-weighted mean (no 1/π, `ibl.wgsl:91-109`), the split-sum
  specular, the GI bake's `a · (e + direct)` and the injection's `outgoing_at` all share the
  premultiplied, π-free diffuse convention. The SH-L1 encode (`3 · mean(f · dir)`) and the
  cosine-convolved decode (`A0 = 1`, `A1 = 2/3`) check out: an open-sky probe reconstructs to 1.0
  for an up-facing surface.
- **IBL.** The cube-face mapping matches the D3D/Vulkan convention on all six faces. The GGX
  importance sampling, Karis's `k = α/2` for the LUT, the roughness↔mip mapping (linear on both
  sides) and the content-hash cache (re-prefilters only on a sky colour change) are all correct.
- **Material colour spaces.** Base colour and emissive upload as `Rgba8UnormSrgb`; MR, normal and
  occlusion as `Rgba8Unorm` (`render.rs:1254-1270`, `material_maps`). Mip generation renders through
  an sRGB view for sRGB maps. Sprite premultiplication is done in linear light. The MR packing
  (G roughness, B metallic) and occlusion in R match glTF. `normal_scale` scales XY only.
- **Mesh asset cache.** The key folds in every texture's content hash (`mesh_asset.rs:52-73`), so
  an edited PNG rebuilds the material bind group.
- **Normals and handedness.** Normals use the per-instance inverse-transpose, tangents the model
  matrix, and `fade.w` flips handedness under reflection (worked through for `diag(-1, 1, 1)`).
  Mirrored draws use the `Cw` pipeline twin in the mesh, shadow and prepass pipelines.
- **Clustered culling.** Tile bounds use the conservative AABB-corner hull with a `w > 0` guard and
  full-tile fallback; shell bounds come from the triangle inequality with a 1% margin. Indices stay
  in ascending light order through the counting sort, extract order is ascending `EntityId`, and the
  CPU `tile_of`/`shell_of` mirror the shader's clamp-then-truncate. The GI light bin
  (`gi_bin.rs`) is likewise conservative, with edge-clamped cells.
- **Shadow plumbing.** The comparison sampler is `LessEqual` with clamp-to-edge; a zeroed
  `light_space` takes the `w == 0` early-out; skinned meshes cast their deformed pose; masking
  shadows off keeps the sun lighting the scene.
- **Quality tiers.** Shafts are masked whenever the shadow map is (`graph.rs:565`). A `pcf` override
  must be odd and non-zero (`ludion-engine/src/quality.rs:468`). Every tier's GI ray count is a
  multiple of `HIT_RAYS`. The clusters are built at `size.render`, so `render_scale` and `--scale`
  stay consistent with the shader's tile lookup.
- **Post stack.** AgX and Neutral floor their inputs before `log2`/division. The fog's optical depth
  clamps its exponents and saturates cleanly. The grade guards its reciprocal gamma. Dithering
  happens in the encoded domain. The SSAO and shaft marches are deterministic (Bayer/R2, no RNG).
- **Bindings.** `shader_gen.rs` drift is gated in both `check.mjs` and CI (`ci/scripts/shader-drift.sh`).
  `ludion-shader-bindgen` rejects a same-named struct with a different layout (`StructConflict`),
  which covers `gi_inject.wgsl`'s second `struct Light`.
- **GI inject.** Its face codes match the bake's `axis * 2 + positive`, and only `GiDynamic` lights
  are injected while the bake takes the static rest, so nothing is counted twice.
